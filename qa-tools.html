Whitespace Cleanup

Case Conversion

Readability Analyzer

A rough, Hemingway-style pass: flags long/complex sentences, likely passive voice, adverb-heavy phrasing, and estimates a Flesch-Kincaid grade level. This is an approximation from a simple syllable heuristic, not a claim to match any commercial tool's exact scoring. Spelling is flagged by your browser's own built-in spell-checker (underlined in the box below) rather than a bundled dictionary.
